How to use

PRIMARY HAND object

  1. Open palm – the model appears above it
  2. Move your hand – the model follows
  3. Close fist – it bursts into star particles
  4. Reopen – the stars reassemble it

SECONDARY HAND controls

  1. Pinch thumb + index – scale up
  2. Spread thumb + index – scale down
  3. Close fist – switch to the next model
  4. Move your hand – rotate the model

Show one hand first – it becomes the primary. The second hand you raise is the secondary.
